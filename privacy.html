mnemo privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

Who

Mnemo is a personal tool used only by K. S. Karakostas on his own computer. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

Read-only access to calendar events of the owner's own Google accounts (scope calendar.events.readonly): each event's title, times, location, description text, video-link fields, and the attendees' display names and response status.

How it uses it

To show a Record / Skip prompt shortly before a video meeting and to label the recording.

Storage

On the owner's computer only. The sign-in grant is stored in a file readable only by the owner.

Sharing

Data is never sold or shared. An event's title and attendee names, saved with a recording, may be part of the text the owner sends to AI providers to transcribe and take notes on his own recordings.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
